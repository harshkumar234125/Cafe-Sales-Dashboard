 Harsh Café House Performance Dashboard 📊

This interactive Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Harsh Café House's sales performance across multiple dimensions such as revenue, orders, products, customers, cities, outlets, and sales trends.

📌 Key Metrics:

• Total Revenue: 3.58M
• Total Orders: 29.2K
• Items Sold: 48.6K
• Avg. Items per Order: 1.66

📊 Dashboard Features:

✅ Page 1: Sales Performance

• KPIs for Revenue, Orders, and Quantity Sold
• Revenue & Sales trend by Month
• Sales performance by Product Category
• Product-wise Revenue analysis
• Weekday vs. Weekend sales performance
• Sales & order trend analysis

✅ Page 2: Customer & Product Analysis

• New vs. Returning Customer analysis
• Customer contribution to Revenue
• Product-wise Sales %
• Top-performing Products
• Revenue by Product Category
• Customer purchasing behavior
• Average Items per Order

✅ Page 3: Location Performance
• Revenue by City
• Revenue by Outlet
• Quantity Sold by City
• Top-performing Cities and Outlets
• City-wise sales comparison
• Outlet performance analysis

🛠️ Tools Used:

• Power BI
• DAX 
• Power Query
• Data Modeling
• Excel

✅  Key Insights:

📈 Cold Beverages generated the highest category revenue at 816K.
📈 Club Sandwich was the top-performing product with approximately 208K in sales.
📈 Returning customers contributed 62.4% of total sales.
📈 Wednesday was the strongest-performing day with approximately 518K in revenue.
📈 Mumbai generated the highest city-level revenue at approximately 1.06M.
📈 Mumbai – Bandra was the top-performing outlet with ₹583K in revenue.

Project Goal: To provide actionable insights into sales performance, customer behavior, product demand, and location performance, helping café management identify key revenue drivers and opportunities for business growth.
